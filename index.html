Privacy Policy

Attendance App

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the Attendance App collects, uses, stores, and protects information.

2. Information We Collect

The app may collect or store the following information:

  • Student Name
  • Roll Number
  • Attendance records
  • Attendance percentage
  • Class or subject information, if entered by the user
  • Information entered manually by the user

We only use this information to provide attendance-related features.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information entered into the app may be used to:

  • Record and manage attendance
  • Calculate attendance percentage
  • Display student attendance records
  • Generate or save attendance-related reports and PDFs
  • Provide the features and functionality of the app

4. Data Storage

Information may be stored locally on your device. If a future version of the app uses an online database or cloud service, the relevant information will be handled according to that service's security and privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We will not share your information with third parties unless it is necessary to provide a requested service, required by law, or necessary to protect the security and rights of the app and its users.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored by the app. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also keep their device secure and avoid sharing their device or app data with unauthorized persons.

7. Data Deletion

If the app provides a delete option, users can delete the information stored within the app using that option.

If you need assistance with deleting your data, you may contact the app developer using the contact information provided in the app.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general educational and attendance-management purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the app's functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the app developer through the contact information provided in the app.

11. Consent

By using the Attendance App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
